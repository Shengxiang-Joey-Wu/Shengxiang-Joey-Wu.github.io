Experience
======
* **Physicist / Scientist**<br><span class="cv-meta">Applied Materials, Santa Clara, CA · Mar&nbsp;2026&nbsp;–&nbsp;Present</span>
  * **Tool development:** Contribute to new-technology nanoimprint and inkjet process-tool development, from concept and component sourcing through rapid prototyping and early-stage validation; review designs for next-generation imprint and inkjet chambers.
  * **Hardware & controls:** Designed and built a 3-channel load-cell assembly for in situ imprint-force monitoring under the wafer chuck; migrated data acquisition from a standalone Keyence DAQ to the tool's native Beckhoff control platform, enabling future closed-loop force control.
  * **Process characterization:** Characterized nanoimprint process mechanics by calibrating imprint force against axis-control setpoints and mapping force distribution across the wafer chuck; identified nonuniformity caused by chuck misleveling and established minimum-force conditions for efficient nanoscale pattern transfer.

* **Process Engineer – Optical Metrology, AR/VR Components (Contract)**<br><span class="cv-meta">Meta Reality Labs, Redmond, WA · May&nbsp;2024&nbsp;–&nbsp;Mar&nbsp;2026</span>
  * **Simulation:** Applied Lumerical FDTD to evaluate the sensitivity of AR waveguide scattering and flare to bondline geometry, material properties, illumination conditions, and multilayer coatings; identified a nonzero bondline-height target that minimizes flare while accommodating manufacturing constraints.
  * **Metrology:** Characterized geometric and surface-relief grating (SRG) AR waveguides, linking physical structure to optical performance: flatness, TTV, and optical wedge by Zygo and ÄPRE interferometry; bondline thickness and roughness by Keyence profilometry validated against AFM; UV–Vis–NIR spectrophotometry; and BSDF scatterometry. Defined key metrics aligning the Redmond site with the supplier factory.
  * **Automation:** Built Python and MATLAB pipelines automating waveguide metrology data analysis, cutting analysis time by more than 50% (over 70% on some tools); packaged the code as standalone MATLAB apps for one-click use by non-programmers and created an interactive web app for human-in-the-loop outlier review.

* **Postdoctoral Researcher – Nanophotonics & Optical Characterization**<br><span class="cv-meta">Emory University, Atlanta, GA · Jan&nbsp;2023&nbsp;–&nbsp;Apr&nbsp;2024</span>
  * **Simulation:** Introduced COMSOL Multiphysics to the group and used it to design high-Q mid-infrared metasurfaces, in both reflection and transmission, with resonances tuned to molecular vibrational modes for cavity-coupling studies.
  * **Optical setup:** Designed and built a Raman spectroscopy system from scratch, including the free-space optical layout, alignment, and calibration.

* **Postdoctoral Researcher**<br><span class="cv-meta">Beijing Computational Science Research Center, Beijing, China · Jan&nbsp;2021&nbsp;–&nbsp;Oct&nbsp;2022</span>
  * **Modeling:** Developed a MATLAB model of plasmon-driven photocatalysis on silver nanoparticles from the ground up: computed the optical response with Mie theory (cross-checked against Lumerical FDTD), derived the plasmon-induced hot-electron and hot-hole energy distributions, and coupled them to a vibrational-heating framework to predict reaction rates in close agreement with published experiments.

* **Graduate Research Assistant**<br><span class="cv-meta">Texas A&M University, College Station, TX · Sep&nbsp;2015&nbsp;–&nbsp;Dec&nbsp;2020</span>
  * **Design & fabrication:** Designed, fabricated, and experimentally validated broadband plasmonic metasurfaces, completing the full device-development cycle from Lumerical FDTD optimization through electron-beam lithography and optical characterization; achieved >90% absorption across 350–800 nm, with measured spectra closely matching simulation.
